Welcome to Santa Monica Detailers

Your go-to place for top-notch car detailing services. We specialize in making your car shine inside and out!

Who We Are

At Santa Monica Detailers, we are passionate about cars and committed to providing the highest level of detailing services. What began as an idea shared between two childhood friends in September 2024 quickly grew into a fully-fledged business focused on delivering professional car care services across the Santa Monica area. Our founders, driven by a shared love of cars and a desire to create something of their own, saw an opportunity to bring top-notch car detailing services to the community. From the very beginning, we’ve worked tirelessly to build a business that values quality, reliability, and customer satisfaction.

As a locally owned business, we take pride in our roots. We believe that every car deserves a personalized touch, whether it’s a simple exterior wash or a complete, meticulous detailing job. We understand that your car is more than just a mode of transportation; it’s a reflection of you. That's why we approach every service with care, attention to detail, and a focus on achieving the best results for our clients. Our goal is to make sure that every car we service not only looks immaculate but also feels refreshed and rejuvenated.

Our Story

The story of Santa Monica Detailers began in the heart of Santa Monica, where our two founders, driven by their entrepreneurial spirit and a passion for cars, decided to take their love for vehicles and transform it into a business. Both founders had always enjoyed working on cars as a hobby, but it wasn’t until 2024 that they decided to turn that passion into a professional service. They recognized the demand for high-quality, eco-friendly detailing services in their local area and set out to meet that need, offering a mobile service that brings the detailing directly to you.

From the very first service, Santa Monica Detailers aimed to stand out from the competition by offering personalized, premium services that put customer satisfaction first. Our founders wanted to create a company where attention to detail wasn’t just a catchphrase but the very essence of the business. With eco-friendly products, a customer-first mentality, and a relentless commitment to perfection, Santa Monica Detailers quickly became a trusted name in the community.

Our Services

We specialize in a range of services designed to keep your car in top condition, inside and out. From quick washes to deep cleanings, our team is equipped with the best tools and products to give your vehicle the care it deserves. We offer both exterior and interior detailing options, as well as special treatments for pet owners, like our dog hair removal service.

  • Exterior Wash: Clean and shine your car's exterior to restore its brilliance.
  • Full Interior & Exterior Detailing: A complete, thorough cleaning of every part of your vehicle, inside and out.
  • Dog Hair Removal: Specialized tools and techniques to remove stubborn pet hair from your car's upholstery and carpeting.
  • Interior Deep Cleaning: Detailed cleaning of your seats, carpets, upholstery, and surfaces to leave your car smelling fresh and looking brand new.
  • Eco-Friendly Products: We use the best environmentally friendly products to ensure both your car and the planet are taken care of.
